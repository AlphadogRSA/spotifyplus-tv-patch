# Experimental lyrics retired in 1.3.0.9

The unstable experimental Spotify+ lyrics feature was removed to simplify setup and maintenance. The custom menu entry, local LRC parser/import tool, LRCLIB client, dialogs and playback-metadata polling are no longer shipped or run. No lyrics-provider requests are made by the extension.

The update does not clear app data or delete previously imported files. Those files and old preferences are unused. Earlier release tags preserve the historical source and notices. This removal concerns the extension feature; Spotify controls any lyrics in its original client.
