Hush companion integration

The MIT Spotify+ TV extension communicates with a separately installed Hush TV companion.
The companion is derived from coldMEW/Hush 2.0.2, commit
4e9ded397338de5c69b1784c636feb3d34a8de01, under GPL-3.0.

Upstream: https://github.com/coldMEW/Hush
Adaptation, pinned source, modifications and build instructions:
https://github.com/AlphadogRSA/spotifyplus-tv-patch/tree/feat/hush-tv/hush

The companion includes the full upstream GPL license and a source notice.
The companion's license does not relicense Spotify's proprietary binaries.
